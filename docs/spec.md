# PRABHAV.exe — Product Specification

## Vision
Transform Prabhav Narayanan's developer and musical portfolio into an interactive vintage CRT television experience combined with Y2K editorial aesthetics ("Connected to the past. Designed for the future.").

## Target Users
- Tech Recruiters & Engineering Hiring Managers seeking creative frontend / full-stack talent.
- Fellow Developers, Creative Technologists, and Collaborators.
- Musicians, Audio Enthusiasts, and Clients.

## Core Experience
1. **Interactive TV Interface**: Central CRT display with curved screen geometry, acid-lime background (`#D9E32A`), deep black typography (`#0A0A0A`), scanlines, phosphor glow, channel transitions with CRT flicker and synthesized retro audio feedback.
2. **Y2K Editorial Outer Shell**: Full-screen Electric Cobalt Blue (`#2F43FC`), distress grain textures, retro star graphics, black-and-white cutout portrait with yellow outline, floating stickers, and physical TV control elements.
3. **6 Broadcast Channels**:
   - **CH 01 [HOME]**: Hero introduction, cutout portrait, animated punchy tagline ("CREATIVE DEVELOPER. DIGITAL EXPERIMENTER. MUSICIAN."), status indicator, quick action triggers.
   - **CH 02 [ABOUT]**: Narrative bio, 2022–2025 developer milestones, leadership & college initiatives, performer-engineer philosophy.
   - **CH 03 [PROJECTS]**: Broadcast showcases for 6 flagship projects (GIGmate, Design Desk, Auralex, Freefolks Band Website, Retro Wallpaper Hub, Permissions RIT) with live links, GitHub repos, tags, and retro broadcast cards.
   - **CH 04 [TECH STACK]**: Interactive skills matrix (Frontend, Backend, AI & Emerging Tech, Tools) with analog signal meters, digital gauge bars, and pixel badges.
   - **CH 05 [FREQUENCIES]**: Musical identity, Violin/Guitar/Keyboard proficiency meters, RITM & Freefolks spotlights, interactive retro audio synthesizer / frequency player.
   - **CH 06 [CONTACT]**: Vintage transmission terminal, email, phone, location, GitHub, direct message broadcast simulator.
4. **Navigation & Controls**:
   - Channel Switcher Knob / Buttons (1 to 6)
   - Menu Overlay (TV Guide modal)
   - Back / Previous Channel button
   - Sound FX synthesizer toggle (clicks, hum, static using Web Audio API — zero heavy asset load)
   - CRT Effects toggle (scanlines, screen warp, flicker)
   - Keyboard shortcuts (1-6 for channels, M for menu, S for sound)
5. **Responsiveness**: Fully responsive desktop editorial layout + streamlined mobile retro pocket-TV layout.
